# Product Plan — English Buddy AI

Audience: Hindi-medium students, Classes 1–8.

Core loop:
Choose class → learn 5–10 minutes → listen → speak → quiz → track progress.

MVP screens:
1. Home / class selection
2. Lesson
3. Speak & Listen
4. Quiz
5. Progress
6. AI Tutor

AI tutor behavior:
- Explain difficult English in simple Hindi.
- Use age-appropriate examples.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Correct mistakes kindly.
- Avoid overwhelming grammar terminology.
- Give a short practice exercise after each explanation.

Voice:
- Default: Indian English (en-IN) where available.
- Calm, clear, slower-than-normal speaking speed.
- A natural male voice depends on the device/browser's available TTS voices; the starter app does not bundle a specific voice.

Safety:
- No public student profiles.
- Minimize collection of children's personal data.
- For a production service, implement parental/privacy requirements appropriate to the countries where it is offered.
